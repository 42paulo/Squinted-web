Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying pre-owned frames and reglazing in the UK.

Is it safe and legal to buy second-hand glasses frames in the UK?

Yes, completely. Spectacle frames are durable accessories designed to last. Buying pre-owned frames and having an optician fit new custom lenses is 100% legal, safe, and significantly more affordable than buying new retail frames.

How do I make sure pre-owned glasses frames will fit me?

Check the inside temple arm of your current comfortable glasses for three numbers (e.g., 52-18-140) representing lens width, bridge width, and temple length in millimetres. Squinted requires sellers to publish these exact measurements on every listing.

How much does it cost to put new prescription lenses into second-hand frames?

Basic single-vision prescription reglazing in the UK typically costs between £15 and £40 through online reglazing specialists or high-street opticians. For complete details on how reglazing works, see our Prescription & Re-Lensing Guide.

What if the frames arrive damaged or not as described?

Every transaction is covered by Squinted Buyer Protection. If your frames arrive damaged, counterfeit, or significantly different from the listing description, report it within 3 days to receive a full refund.
