Squinted Community Guidelines

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to the clearest corner of the internet! We’re a community of frame-fixated, budget-savvy, and eco-conscious shoppers. To keep our marketplace looking sharp, we’ve got a few "house rules" to follow.

  • Be "Crystal Clear"
  • Honesty is our favourite look. If you’re selling, describe your frames exactly as they are—scratches, loose hinges, and all. If you’re buying, ask all the questions you need! Clear communication means nobody is left squinting at the truth.
  • Keep it Friendly (No Shady Behaviour)
  • We’re all here because we love a good pair of specs. Treat your fellow members with respect. Rude comments or aggressive haggling are definitely out of style. If you wouldn't say it to someone’s face (while they’re wearing your favourite frames), don't say it in the messages!
  • No "Blurry" Listings
  • We’re a dedicated eyewear marketplace, not a junk drawer!
  • Do: Post spectacles and sunglasses - prescription or non-prescription
  • Don't: Post your old sneakers, your cat, or anything that isn't eyewear-related. We want to keep the feed focused so users don't have to squint to find what they’re looking for.
  • Respect the "Frame"
  • We’re a community built on trust. Keep your transactions and conversations inside the Squinted platform. It’s the only way we can protect your payments and make sure everything stays in focus for both the buyer and the seller.
  • Help Each Other Out
  • See a fellow member asking about a bridge width you recognise? Or maybe someone needs advice on where to get a "Fair" frame repaired? Feel free to share the knowledge! We’re all here to help each other see better for less.
  • See something "Out of Focus"? If you spot a listing that looks fake, a user being unkind, or someone trying to skirt the rules, send us an email. Our team will take a look so the rest of the community doesn't have to squint at the drama.

If you have questions regarding our compliance definitions or structural policies, reach out to the Squinted administration team at support@squinted.co.uk.
