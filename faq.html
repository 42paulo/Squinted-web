General Questions

How do I put my personal prescription lenses into frames I buy here?

It’s incredibly simple. Once your pre-owned frames arrive, you can take them directly to any high-street optician or use a trusted online re-lensing service. They will simply cut and fit a fresh pair of lenses matching your custom prescription directly into the frame structure.

What are "Perfect Match" Alerts?

If you’re looking for a highly specific frame size or measurement configuration, you can save your specs inside the app. Perfect Match works in the background—the moment another user lists a set of glasses matching your target data, we'll ping your device instantly.

Selling on Squinted

What is the "Magnify" feature?

Magnify is our premium listing boost tool. For a small fee, you can supercharge your frames to pin them to the absolute top of the user feed for 7 days, guaranteeing maximum impressions and higher chances of a speedy transaction.

How do payments and shipping work?

All checkouts are managed via our integrated Stripe Connect portal for absolute payment security. Shipping parameters are entirely standardized across the platform, giving sellers trackable, zero-guesswork postage options to ship items safely.
