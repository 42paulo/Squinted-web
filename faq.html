General Questions

How do I put my personal prescription lenses into frames I buy here?

It’s incredibly simple. Once your pre-owned frames arrive, you can take them directly to any high-street optician or use a trusted online re-lensing service. They will simply cut and fit a fresh pair of lenses matching your custom prescription directly into the frame structure.

What are "Perfect Match" Alerts?

If you’re looking for a highly specific frame size or measurement configuration, you can save your specs inside the app. Perfect Match works in the background—the moment another user lists a set of glasses matching your target data, we'll ping your device instantly.

What if the frames arrive and they just don't suit my face?

We’ve all been there—sometimes a frame looks great on the screen but "not quite me" in the mirror. While we encourage sellers to provide all the measurements so you aren't squinting at a tiny frame on a big head, individual return policies are set by each seller. Check the listing details before you buy! If they don't work out, you can always relist them on Squinted and keep the circle going.

How do I know the frames are authentic?

Nobody wants to be fooled by a "faux-go." We ask our sellers to provide clear "Squint-Shots" of the branding on the inner arms and any original packaging. Because we are a community of eyewear enthusiasts, our users help keep things sharp. If something looks blurry or "off," our team is here to take a closer look.

Are the lenses included in the price?

Yes, you’re buying the whole package! However, since most pre-owned frames come with the previous owner’s prescription, we usually recommend our "Re-lensing 101" trick. Unless you lucky enough to be notified by Squinted of a "Perfect Match", you’ll likely want to pop in your own lenses.

Is it safe to buy "well-loved" frames?

Absolutely! One person’s "Fair" is another person’s "Vintage Treasure." We use our Condition Guide to make sure there are no surprises. If you’re looking for a project or a backup pair for the beach, "Good" or "Fair" frames are a total steal. If you want that "just-left-the-optician" feeling, stick to "Mint."

How much does it cost to re-lens?

It’s usually way cheaper than buying a whole new setup! Most local opticians or online lens-mounters charge a flat fee for the lenses and the fitting. You’re essentially getting designer frames for a fraction of the cost, then just paying for the "glass" that helps you see. No squinting at the bill!"

How do I know if the size is right?

Check the "Measurements" section on the listing! We’ve taught our sellers how to find those three little numbers (Lens, Bridge, Temple) and our AI engine automatically populates them. If you have a pair of glasses at home that fits you perfectly, compare the numbers. If they match, you’re golden!"
