Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about listing and selling frames in the UK.

Where is the best place to sell prescription glasses in the UK?

Squinted is a dedicated marketplace for pre-owned eyewear. Unlike general selling sites, Squinted only offers glasses and accessories. So you'll be attracting buyers specifically looking for pre-loved glasses to give a second life to.

Do I need to remove prescription lenses before selling my glasses?

No. You can leave existing lenses fitted. Simply state in your listing whether the fitted lenses are prescription, non-prescription (plano), or sunglasses. Buyers will take the frames to an optician or online reglazing service to have new lenses fitted. For complete details, see our Prescription & Re-Lensing Guide.

How much does it cost to list frames on Squinted?

Listing your frames on Squinted is 100% free. You set your own listing price and keep your agreed selling price when your item is bought.

What types of eyewear can I sell?

You can list pre-owned spectacle frames, designer sunglasses, vintage frames, and optical accessories from any brand (such as Ray-Ban, Oliver Peoples, Tom Ford, Prada, Oakley, or high-street optician frames).

We don't trade in contact lenses, prescription lens-only orders, or non-eyewear items.
