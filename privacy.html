2. The Personal Data We Collect

2.1 We collect and process the following categories of personal data about you:

  • (a) Identity and account data: your name, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or over), and login credentials (passwords are held in encrypted form).
  • Contact data: your email address, telephone number and, where you buy or sell, your postal and delivery address.
  • (c) Listing and profile data: the content of your Listings, including item descriptions, photographs and any specification or prescription figures you choose to include, together with your profile, preferences and saved items.
  • (d) Transaction data: details of your purchases, sales, Buyer Protection Fees and payout history. We do not store your full payment card details, which are processed by Stripe; we may hold limited reference data such as the last four digits and bank name.
  • (e) Seller verification and payout data: where you sell, the identity, bank/payout and (where required) tax information collected to onboard you for payouts and to meet our legal obligations. Much of this is collected and held by Stripe as part of its onboarding.
  • (f)Communications data: messages you send through the Platform (including messages between buyers and sellers), and your correspondence with us.
  • (g) Usage, technical and device data: how you access and use the Platform, IP address (in pseudonymised form), device and browser information, and data collected through cookies (see our Cookie Policy).
  • (h) Marketing and preferences data: your marketing and communication preferences.

2.2 Special category data. We do not seek to collect special category data (such as health data). Any prescription or optical figures you include in a Listing are provided by you and relate to the item; you should not include health information about yourself or any other person in a Listing or message. Where any special category data is provided to us, we process it only so far as necessary and, where required, with your explicit consent. Because buyers arrange their own prescription lenses (“relensing”) through a registered optician, any prescription figures in a Listing relate to the item and its frames, not to the health of any buyer.

3. How We Collect Your Personal Data

3.1 Directly. You provide personal data when you register, complete your profile, create Listings, buy or sell, message other users, contact us, or otherwise use the Platform.

3.2 Automatically. We may collect technical and usage data automatically through cookies and similar technologies as you use the Platform. See our Cookie Policy.

From third parties. We receive data from Stripe in connection with payments, seller onboarding and fraud checks; from SendCloud and carriers in connection with shipping and tracking; and from identity-verification and fraud-prevention providers we use to operate the Platform safely.

5. Sharing Your Personal Data

5.1 Between buyers and sellers. Because the Platform is a marketplace, some data is shared between users to enable a sale and delivery. In particular, when you buy, we share your name and delivery address with the seller and carrier so the item can be sent to you; and limited information is shared to enable messaging and, where relevant, a Buyer Protection claim. Each user is responsible, as an independent controller, for the personal data they receive about another user, and must use it only to complete the transaction.

5.2 Our service providers. We share personal data with providers who process it on our behalf or, where indicated, as independent controllers, including: Stripe (payments, seller onboarding and fraud prevention – Stripe acts as an independent controller for its own compliance, verification and fraud-prevention purposes); SendCloud and carriers (shipping and tracking); hosting, IT, identity-verification and fraud-prevention providers; and our professional advisers.

5.3 Others. We may share data with regulators, HMRC, law enforcement and other authorities where required or permitted by law, and with a buyer of our business or assets (in which case personal data may transfer as part of that transaction). We do not sell your personal data.

6. International Transfers

6.1 Some of our providers process personal data outside the United Kingdom. Where we transfer personal data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as transfer to a country the UK has deemed adequate, or use of the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. In particular, we rely on those mechanisms for transfers by Stripe and SendCloud.

7. Data Security

7.1 We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls and regular reviews. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; you are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

8. How Long We Keep Your Personal Data

8.1 We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which we collected it, including to meet legal, accounting, tax and dispute-resolution requirements. In general:

  • (a) account, identity and contact data is kept for the life of your account and for 12 months after closure;
  • (b) transaction, payout and tax records are kept for six years from the end of the relevant tax year, to meet accounting and platform-reporting obligations;
  • (c) Buyer Protection, dispute and communications records are kept for six years; and
  • (d) usage and analytics data is kept in pseudonymised or aggregated form in line with our analytics settings.

9. Cookies

9.1 We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy, which explains the cookies we use and how you can manage your preferences.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

10.1 Subject to the conditions in data protection law, you have the right to: access your personal data; have inaccurate data corrected; have data erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and, where we rely on consent, to withdraw it at any time. You also have the right to complain to the ICO (see clause 1.4).

10.2 To exercise any right, contact us at hello@squinted.co.uk. We do not usually charge a fee, we may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within one month (which we may extend for complex requests, telling you if so).

11. Automated Decision-Making

We may use automated tools to help detect fraud and misuse. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing; if this changes, we will update this Notice and tell you about your rights.

12. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We keep this Privacy Notice under review and may update it from time to time. Where changes are material, we will notify you by email or through the Platform. The “Last updated” date shows when it was last revised.

13. Lawful Bases – What They Mean

13.1 Performance of a contract means processing necessary to perform a contract with you, or to take steps at your request before entering into one[cite: 8].

13.2 Legitimate interests means processing necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party, where those interests are not overridden by your rights[cite: 8].

13.3 Legal obligation means processing necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation to which we are subject[cite: 8].

13.4 Consent means processing based on your specific, informed and freely given agreement, which you may withdraw at any time[cite: 8].
