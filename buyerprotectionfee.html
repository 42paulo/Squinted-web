Buyer Protection Fee

Last Updated: June 2026

In short: a mandatory fee of 5% plus £0.70 is added to every order to fund our Buyer Protection cover. It's shown in the listing price breakdown and again at checkout — no VAT is currently added, as Squinted Ltd is not yet VAT-registered.

Our goal is to provide buyers with a secure purchasing experience by applying a Buyer Protection fee. This fee is mandatory for every order made through Squinted and is added automatically at checkout.

The calculation of this fee varies based on several factors, such as:

  • item characteristics
  • order value

The Buyer Protection fee includes a percentage of the final item price, as well as a fixed fee — in our case 5% plus £0.70.

As you browse items, the fee is included in the price breakdown for each listing and reflected in the total price for the item. At checkout, the total Buyer Protection fee will be added to your order's final price and clearly displayed in the order summary. Squinted Ltd is not currently VAT-registered, so no VAT is charged on this fee. If this changes, this page will be updated and the applicable VAT will be shown separately at checkout.
