Re-lensing 101: Your Style, Your Vision

Last Updated: June 2026

Found a pair of frames that look incredible, but the prescription is all wrong? Don’t start squinting in frustration! One of the best-kept secrets in the eyewear world is that frames are forever, but lenses are swappable. Think of it like buying a vintage car and putting in a brand-new engine. Here’s how to do it:

  • The Find

    • Scour Squinted for that designer shape or vintage vibe you’ve been dreaming of. Don't worry about the lenses currently in them—you’re just looking for the "bones" (the frames).

  • The Visit

    • Take your "new-to-you" frames to your local optician or an online lens-replacement service. Most shops are more than happy to "re-lens" a frame for you. It’s a standard service!

  • The Swap

    • They’ll pop out the old lenses, take a digital scan of the frames, and cut fresh lenses specifically for your prescription. Bonus: You can even turn them into sunglasses or blue-light blockers while you're at it!

  • Why Re-lens?

    • Major Savings: Even with the cost of new lenses, you’re usually saving hundreds compared to buying the same designer frames brand-new. The Perfect Fit: You get the high-end quality of a luxury brand with the precision of your current prescription. Eco-Friendly: You’re keeping a perfectly good set of frames out of a landfill. That’s a win for your wallet and the planet.

Stop the Squint: If you find a pair you love, don't let someone else's prescription stop you. As long as the frames are in "Great" or "Good" condition, they’re ready for a second life on your face!
