How It Works

We’re not opticians.

We’re not here to test your eyes, prescribe your lenses or tell you which frames suit you.

Squinted is a marketplace for previously owned glasses frames.

A place to buy and sell frames that have lots of life left in them — giving fantastic frames a second chance to be loved in a more affordable way.

Squinted. Pre-loved frames. A new sustainable way to find your next pair. An eyewear revolution!

Here’s how Squinted works

1

List with an AI Assist

Got frames gathering dust? Snap a few bright photos and let our built-in AI Frame Screening handle the heavy lifting to help you list your spectacles or sunglasses in seconds.

Turning your old glasses into cash has never been this smooth. Find out more in our Guide to Selling Used Glasses.

2

Live AR Try-On & Perfect Match Alerts

Browse the marketplace and virtually test out frames on your own face in real-time with our AR Try-On.

Save your prescription details and let Perfect Match do the digging. The moment a frame hits our listings that matches your exact prescription, we’ll ping you instantly so you never miss out.

Premium Boost
3 ✨

Magnify Your Listing

Want to sell even faster? Boost your frames with Magnify. For a small fee, give your listing prime placement at the top of the search feed for 7 days to get maximum eyeballs and a quick sale.

4

Lens & Go

Once your new frames arrive, the journey doesn't stop there. Simply take them to your favourite high-street optician or an online re-lensing specialist to have your custom prescription fitted.

Getting a fresh set of lenses is quick, affordable, and the ultimate way to unlock a frame's second chance. Read our Guide to Reglazing Glasses for full step-by-step details.

Got more questions about buying pre-owned frames? Check out Buy Used Prescription Glasses & Designer Frames for more info.
